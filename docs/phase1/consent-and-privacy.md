# Learner Video Recording & Data Privacy Consent Policy

**Project:** AI Vocational Skills Trainer  
**Applies To:** Vocational trainees, instructors, and partner technical institutions  
**Version:** 1.0 (Draft)  

---

## 1. Plain-Language Summary

We take your personal privacy and safety very seriously. As part of your vocational training coursework, you will record short videos of your physical workbench wiring exercises and upload them to our platform. These videos are analyzed by our automated system and reviewed by your course instructors to give you detailed, step-by-step guidance, help you correct mistakes early, and verify that your electrical work is completely safe.

You have clear rights regarding what is recorded, who can see it, and how your data is protected.

---

## 2. What Data Is Collected and Stored

When you participate in practice video assessments, the platform collects and securely stores the following data:

1. **Practice Video & Audio Footage:** The video recording you submit, showing your hands, tools, components, practice board, and any ambient audio recorded during the exercise.
2. **Identity & Academic Metadata:** Your full name, trainee enrollment ID, email address, training batch/class, and affiliated vocational institution.
3. **Assessment Results & AI Annotations:** 
   - Timestamps identifying when specific steps were performed.
   - Automated step-level classification (`Pass`, `Fail`, `Inconclusive`).
   - Detected procedural issues (e.g., stripped wire length, terminal tightness indicator).
   - Instructor comments, grade adjustments, and written feedback.
4. **Technical Audit Logs:** Upload timestamps, file size, IP address, device browser type, and consent audit records.

---

## 3. Who Can Access Your Data

Access to your recordings and evaluations is strictly restricted on a need-to-know basis through Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC):

- **You (The Learner):** Full access to view your own submitted videos, step-by-step AI feedback, and trainer scores.
- **Your Assigned Trainers / Instructors:** Authorized to inspect your submissions, evaluate steps, annotate mistakes, override scores, and leave feedback.
- **Your Institution Administrators:** Authorized to view aggregated completion rates, safety compliance reports, and audit trails for academic accreditation.
- **Authorized Technical Development Team:** Restricted, auditable access solely for maintaining system reliability, troubleshooting upload errors, and retraining AI models on de-identified practice samples.

*Your video recordings are never sold, rented, publicly published, or shared with commercial third-party advertisers.*

---

## 4. How Long Data Is Retained (Retention Policy)

1. **Active Academic Period:** All raw video files and assessment logs are maintained in high-speed secure cloud storage throughout your active academic semester or vocational training year.
2. **Accreditation Audit Retention (+6 Months):** Upon completion of your training term, records are maintained for an additional **six (6) months** to satisfy mandatory vocational training board audits and grade moderation.
3. **Archival & Anonymization:** Following the audit window, raw video recordings containing facial or background identifiers are permanently deleted from primary storage. De-identified, anonymized procedural clips (showing only workbench hands and wires without student faces or identifiers) may be retained in our research benchmark repository solely for improving vocational AI accuracy, unless you explicitly request full deletion.

---

## 5. Security & Storage Safeguards

- **Encryption in Transit:** All video uploads and platform interactions occur over encrypted channels using TLS 1.3.
- **Encryption at Rest:** All video files, database records, and backups are stored using AES-256 standard encryption on secure servers.
- **Access Logs:** Every access, view, or download of a student recording is logged in an immutable security audit trail.

---

## 6. Your Rights & How to Withdraw Consent

Your participation in video-assisted evaluation is designed to enhance your learning. However, you maintain the following rights:

1. **Right to Withdraw:** You may withdraw your consent for future video recording at any time by notifying your instructor or submitting a written request to your institution’s training office.
2. **Alternate Assessment Method:** If you choose not to record video or withdraw your consent, you will **not** be penalized academically. Instead, your practical assessments will be scheduled for traditional, in-person live observation by an instructor during designated workshop hours.
3. **Right to Deletion:** Upon graduation or course exit, you may request the complete deletion of your identifiable video recordings from platform storage, subject to institutional retention regulations.
