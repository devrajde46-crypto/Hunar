# Practice Video Filming Guidelines for Learners

**Target Trade:** Basic Electrical Wiring  
**Document Purpose:** Clear, practical guidelines for vocational learners recording workbench practical exercises using standard smartphones for AI evaluation and instructor review.

---

## 1. Camera Setup & Mounting

1. **Use a Stable Mount:**
   - **Never hold the phone by hand or ask a friend to hold it.** Handheld video causes camera shake, orientation changes, and blocks crucial terminal views.
   - Use a simple workbench clamp, desktop phone tripod, or stable weighted stand placed directly on the workbench.
2. **Camera Orientation:**
   - Always record in **Landscape orientation (horizontal, 16:9)**.
   - Ensure camera resolution is set to **1080p (Full HD) at 30 fps** (or minimum 720p). Do not use 4K (file size becomes too large to upload easily).
3. **Camera Angle:**
   - Mount the camera at an angle of **35° to 45° above the practice board**, looking down and slightly forward.
   - This overhead angle allows the camera to see inside switch boxes, behind terminals, and down onto terminal screw heads without your forearms blocking the lens.
4. **Distance & Framing:**
   - Place the camera **50 cm to 70 cm (approx. 2 feet)** away from the center of the practice board.
   - Frame the shot so that:
     - The entire practice board is in view.
     - Your hands, hand tools, and wire ends remain inside the frame at all times.
     - The power supply cord plug and multimeter screen are clearly visible.

---

## 2. Lighting & Visual Clarity

1. **Bright, Direct Lighting:**
   - Ensure the workbench is brightly lit from above or slightly in front.
   - Use an overhead tube light or an adjustable desk lamp angled directly toward the terminal boxes.
2. **Avoid Backlighting & Glare:**
   - Never position the camera facing an open window or bright light source behind the board (this silhouetted image makes wire colors unrecognizable).
   - Watch out for reflections on glossy plastic switch surfaces or multimeter glass screens.
3. **Contrast:**
   - Use a clean, non-reflective workbench surface (wooden board, light grey or blue ESD mat) so dark wires (Black/Blue) and light wires (Red/Yellow) stand out with sharp contrast.

---

## 3. Framing Hands, Tools, and Terminal Details

The AI evaluates fine mechanical skills (wire stripping quality, copper insertion depth, terminal tightness). Help the camera see your work:

1. **Do Not Block the Lens with Shoulders or Forearms:**
   - Keep your elbows relaxed and work from the side of the camera line rather than directly leaning between the lens and the workpiece.
2. **The "2-Second Inspection Hold":**
   - After stripping a conductor, hold the stripped wire end still in front of the terminal box for **2 seconds** before inserting it. This allows the camera autofocus to capture clean evidence of zero cut strands and proper strip length.
   - After tightening any screw, perform a deliberate tug test and hold still for **2 seconds** so the camera captures the physical resistance.
3. **Show Multimeter Displays:**
   - When taking a voltage or continuity measurement, angle the multimeter screen directly toward the camera so the digits and unit annunciators (e.g., `0.0V` or `< 0.2Ω`) can be clearly read in the video recording.

---

## 4. Video Length & Pacing

1. **Keep Total Duration Under Control:**
   - Target video length: **5 to 8 minutes** for complete procedures.
   - Do not rush. Smooth, deliberate movements look clearer and are scored more accurately by AI than frantic fast movements.
2. **Continuous Uncut Recording:**
   - Record each procedure in **one single continuous take**.
   - Do not stop, cut, pause, or edit the video mid-task. Edited cuts invalidate safety-critical compliance steps (e.g., proving the board remained disconnected throughout).
3. **Preparation Before Recording:**
   - Lay out all required tools (pliers, strippers, screwdrivers, multimeter) and cut wire lengths on the bench **before** hitting record.

---

## 5. What to Avoid (Red Flags)

- ❌ **No Handheld Shooting:** Any video with excessive motion blur or shaking will be rejected.
- ❌ **No Portrait (Vertical) Videos:** Vertical videos crop out the edges of the practice board.
- ❌ **No Obscured Connections:** Don't cup your hands completely around terminal blocks while tightening screws; keep fingers back on the screwdriver handle.
- ❌ **No Performing Steps Off-Camera:** If you strip a wire or test a terminal outside the camera frame, that step cannot be scored and will be marked `Inconclusive / Failed`.
- ❌ **No Energized Touching:** Never connect the power cord to live mains until all dead checks are recorded and your instructor has inspected the setup.

---

## 6. Phone-Friendly Quick Checklist (5-Point Check)

Before pressing **RECORD**, check these 5 points:

```
[ ] 1. MOUNT:   Phone fixed securely on clamp/tripod in LANDSCAPE mode.
[ ] 2. FRAME:   Entire practice board, power plug, and meter display in frame.
[ ] 3. LIGHT:   Workbench brightly lit with no harsh shadows or backlights.
[ ] 4. CLEAN:   Bench clear of extra junk; tools laid out within easy reach.
[ ] 5. FOCUS:   Tap phone screen on switch terminals to lock autofocus & exposure.
```
