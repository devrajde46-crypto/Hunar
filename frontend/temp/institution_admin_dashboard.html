Data-Privacy & Workshop Video Retention Policy (NCVT & DGT Regulatory Notice)

Statutory Rule § 14-B

Practice video recordings are stored encrypted under Directorate General of Training (DGT) vocational privacy guidelines. High-definition raw bench footage is retained for 90 days following final certified instructor evaluation, after which raw video streams are permanently purged. Anonymized vector keypoint telemetry and signed trainer audit logbooks are securely archived for 3 years for statutory accreditation reviews. Trainee consent records are registered upon workshop induction; no recordings are distributed to public networks or commercial generative datasets.
