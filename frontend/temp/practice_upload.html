Single-Pole Switch Controlling a Batten Lamp

Objective: Wire a single-way SPST (Single Pole Single Throw) switch to control an incandescent or LED batten lamp fixture from a single phase 230V AC supply in accordance with IS 732 / National Electrical Code regulations.

warning Contains 2 Safety-Critical Steps
•
Standard: IS 732: 2019 (Code of Practice for Electrical Wiring)
•
Assessment: Physical Continuity & Video Trace
warning
Mandatory Safety Protocol Life Safety Check

Isolate the supply and prove the circuit dead before touching any conductor

Verify with a calibrated multimeter or approved two-pole voltage tester across Phase-to-Neutral and Phase-to-Earth at the distribution block before stripping, clipping, or terminating conductors. Never rely on an indicator light or switch orientation alone.

Step-by-Step Procedure Instructions

All steps require clear camera line-of-sight
STEP 1

Mounting the Batten Lamp Holder & Switch Box

Mechanical Assembly

Fix the wooden mounting board securely to your lab station rail. Fasten the batten lamp holder base and surface switch box ensuring mechanical rigidity. Check that the switch box orientation places the TOP marking strictly upward to maintain standard down-push-to-close behavior.

videocam
Camera View Required: Wide angle shot showing hands fixing screws and confirming switch box is oriented with "TOP" marking upward without baseboard wobble.
STEP 2

Preparing & Stripping Conductor Insulation

Conductor Prep

Measure and cut the required lengths of 1.5 sq mm copper wire. Using the adjustable wire stripper, strip 8mm to 10mm of PVC insulation from both ends. Ensure the cutting blade does not nick, score, or cut any copper strands, which would create localized high-resistance hot spots under current load.

videocam
Camera View Required: Close-up macro view of the wire stripper action showing intact bare copper core with zero strand shearing and clean square insulation cut.
STEP 3

Phase Wire Termination to Switch Terminal

warningSafety-critical

Terminate the incoming Phase (Live) red conductor directly to the bottom terminal of the SPST switch. Never terminate the neutral wire to the switch. The switch must isolate live line potential so that the fixture is de-energized when switched OFF. Ensure the clamping screw bears down evenly on copper without bare conductor protruding beyond the plastic terminal body.

warningCritical Warning: Wiring a switch into the neutral line leaves the lamp holder permanently alive at 230V even when turned OFF, creating a fatal shock hazard during bulb replacement.
videocam
Camera View Required: Clear unobstructed angle showing the red live wire entering the bottom switch terminal and the screw tightened firmly with zero excess bare conductor showing.
STEP 4

Connecting Switch Return Wire to Lamp Holder

Switched Live Loop

Connect the switch return conductor (phase loop) from the top switch terminal through the conduit or surface pathway to the central pin contact terminal of the batten lamp holder. Fasten the brass terminal set-screw securely using your 3mm flathead insulated screwdriver.

videocam
Camera View Required: Top-down view tracking the continuous conductor path from the upper switch terminal along the mounting board to the lamp holder socket.
STEP 5

Terminating Neutral Conductor to Lamp Holder

Neutral Return

Connect the black incoming neutral conductor directly to the outer threaded / bayonet side terminal of the batten lamp holder. Verify there are no loose stray strands touching the outer plastic shell or metal bezel. Thread the back dome cap and tighten securely over the lamp base.

videocam
Camera View Required: Close-up view of the interior lamp terminal block showing tight clamp on the neutral wire with zero strand slippage before closing holder dome.
STEP 6

Dead Circuit Continuity & Insulation Resistance Test

warningSafety-critical

Before any mains power is applied, perform dead-circuit testing. Set your digital multimeter to continuity/resistance mode (Ω). Verify the switch operation:

Switch in OPEN position: Resistance between supply Phase & lamp terminal must read OL (Infinite ohms).
Switch in CLOSED position: Resistance between supply Phase & lamp center pin must read < 0.5 Ω with audible continuity beep.
videocam
Camera View Required: Stable bench view displaying both multimeter test probes placed firmly on terminals and the digital display readout clearly visible to camera during switch toggle.
